# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Discerning luxury consumers, collectors, and high-net-worth clients in the Philippines (principally Bonifacio Global City / Metro Manila and regional buyers) seeking authentic, curated luxury designer handbags, tote bags, and shoulder bags.

## Product Purpose

Tiffany & Cris is an exclusive curated catalog and private boutique service showcasing luxury designer handbags and accessories from the world's finest ateliers. The application enables clients to browse authentic pieces, inspect multi-angle imagery and specifications, build wishlists, and book private viewing appointments or submit acquisition inquiries.

## Positioning

"Wear elegance like a second skin." A boutique-curated alternative to mass luxury retail, offering high-touch personal concierge viewing, authentic provenance, and private showroom consultations in Bonifacio Global City (Taguig, Metro Manila).

## Operating Context

- **Client Browsing & Inquiry Workflow:** Desktop and mobile web browsing of collections, filtering by category (Tote, Shoulder, Crossbody, Evening), detailed multi-image inspect modal, wishlist curation, and direct inquiry/booking forwarding via form and Facebook/social channels.
- **Boutique Administration:** Secure admin panel for inventory creation, multi-image upload and management, pricing and stock status updates, and viewing request tracking.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Single-Page Application:** Built with React 19 and Vite with fast client-side routing, responsive navigation, and smooth animations.
- **Database & Storage:** Supabase for real-time collection storage, product metadata, and asset references with fallback fetch mechanisms.
- **Visual Presentation:** Rich multi-image carousel viewer, high-contrast dark luxury aesthetic with light theme toggle support.
- **Inquiry-Driven Commerce:** Focus on lead generation, showroom viewings, and concierge inquiries rather than automated anonymous cart checkout.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name & Identity:** Tiffany & Cris | Luxury Designer Bag Collections Manila.
- **Voice & Tone:** Sophisticated, understated, refined, timeless, exclusive.
- **Visual Signature:** Deep obsidian blacks, warm golden accents, champagne neutrals, with classical Cormorant Garamond serif headings paired with clean uppercase Montserrat tracking.

## Product Principles

1. **Craft Over Noise:** The interface recedes to spotlight the handcrafted leather goods and textures.
2. **Effortless Exclusivity:** Clear paths to private consultations and booking without friction or generic e-commerce clutter.
3. **Impeccable Typography & Hierarchy:** High-editorial typographic contrast honoring classical luxury fashion houses.
4. **Resilient Performance:** Instantaneous filtering, preloaded typography, smooth modal interactions, and optimized responsive layouts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- High contrast text options and light/dark theme support.
- Screen-reader accessible semantic HTML, JSON-LD schema metadata, and ARIA labels on interactive modal and carousel controls.
